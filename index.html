About us

A trading tradition built over decades.

Precious Metals (India) Inc. is an international corporate trading business built on a heritage dating back to 1965. Our interests cover precious metals, antique materials, historical collectibles, bronze coins, antique idols, coins and banknotes.

We connect genuine buyers, suppliers, collectors and trading organizations across markets, and aim to build a trusted network that values heritage, commercial integrity and long-term relationships.

Our mission

To facilitate responsible international trade and build strong business relationships in precious metals and antique collectible markets.

Our vision

To become a recognized international trading organization connecting global markets through heritage-based business, valuable materials and professional trading networks.
