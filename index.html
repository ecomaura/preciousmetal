Buyer advisory & compliance

Important legal notice: antique trading in India.

The following notice is provided for general information and awareness. It is not legal advice.

Governing law

The trade, possession, registration and export of antiquities in India are regulated under the Antiquities and Art Treasures Act, 1972 (AATA) and applicable rules, administered by the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI).

Definition of antiquity

  • Objects, sculptures, paintings and works of art or craftsmanship that are at least 100 years old.
  • Manuscripts, records and documents of historical, literary, scientific or artistic interest that are at least 75 years old.

Buying and selling

Commercial dealing in antiquities is subject to applicable licensing requirements. Dealers must hold the appropriate valid licence. Transactions must comply with applicable legal and documentation requirements. Buyers should verify the dealer’s licence, ownership documents, provenance, authenticity and applicable registration certificates before completing a transaction.

Registration requirements

Certain categories of antiquities are subject to compulsory registration. These may include specified sculptures, objects made of stone, metal or ivory, paintings and other notified categories. Possession, transfer or dealing in regulated antiquities without fulfilling applicable registration requirements may attract legal penalties, confiscation or prosecution.

Export restrictions

Export of Indian antiquities is strictly regulated. Private individuals and commercial entities cannot freely export Indian antiquities. Export is prohibited except through legally permitted government-authorized channels and in accordance with applicable law. Vintage and collectible objects must be appropriately assessed before export to determine whether they qualify as antiquities under Indian law.

Buyer advisory and authenticity verification

Items available through local antique markets, private collections, auctions and online platforms may include genuine registered antiquities, vintage objects less than 100 years old, modern reproductions and decorative replicas, or collectibles requiring independent age and authenticity verification. Buyers and sellers are advised to conduct appropriate legal due diligence before entering into any transaction.

Compliance disclaimer

Our organisation supports responsible, transparent and legally compliant trade practices. All antique-related transactions are subject to applicable Indian laws, regulatory approvals, licensing requirements and verification procedures. No item shall be represented, purchased, sold, transported or exported as a legally tradable antiquity without the necessary legal verification and compliance.

Regulatory authority: Archaeological Survey of India (ASI)
Applicable legislation: Antiquities and Art Treasures Act, 1972

This notice is intended for general information and awareness. It does not constitute legal advice. Please consult a qualified legal professional and the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) for transaction-specific guidance.
